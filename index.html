The Wild Kingdom

Welcome! Explore and learn interesting facts about different animals.

Lion

The lion is a large cat of the genus Panthera native to Africa and India. It has a muscular, deep-chested body, short, rounded head, and a hairy tuft at the end of its tail.

Elephant

Elephants are the largest existing land animals. They are characterized by their long proboscis (trunk), tusks, large ear flaps, and massive legs.

Penguin

Penguins are a group of aquatic flightless birds. They live almost exclusively in the Southern Hemisphere, where they catch food while swimming underwater.
